02 — How it works

From lead to library in seven calm steps.

  1. 01

    Select the dog

    Search a household, tap the dog, start the session. It stays selected for every clip that follows.

  2. 02

    Record

    The phone camera opens. Linda films the moment — no forms, no fiddling.

  3. 03

    Upload, automatically

    A resumable upload starts the second recording stops — with honest progress if the connection wobbles.

  4. 04

    AI drafts the notes

    A suggested title, summary, transcript and technique tags — with confidence and evidence. Drafts only.

  5. 05

    Linda reviews

    She edits, trims, and chooses exactly who can see it. Nothing is visible until she does.

  6. 06

    Shared privately

    A secure portal link, ready for WhatsApp. Linda sends it herself from her own WhatsApp.

  7. 07

    Revisit anytime

    Owners rewatch, tap timestamps and tick off homework — long after the session ends.

05 — Privacy by default

Every video starts private.

Nothing leaves Linda’s hands until she chooses who can see it — and public use always needs recorded consent.

Permission model

Four audiences. One gate. Every change audited.

AI drafts. Linda decides.

AI never publishes, grants access or identifies people. It suggests; Linda approves.

Links, not loose files

Secure portal links are the default. Attaching a video file is optional — and comes with a warning, because copies can’t be recalled.

Revocable, auditable

Remove a household member and their access ends. Every grant, approval and consent is logged.
